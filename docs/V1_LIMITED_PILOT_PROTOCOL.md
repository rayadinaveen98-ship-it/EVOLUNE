# EVOLUNE v1 — Limited pilot protocol (prepared, not conducted)

**Drafted:** 2026-11-11. No volunteer has been recruited, tested or enrolled by creating this document.

## Eligibility and consent
- Adult volunteers who choose to test a general self-development app. Diverse Android versions, languages, text sizes, phone/tablet form factors and accessibility needs.
- Voluntary, written/in-app explanation of the app's limits: wellness support, not therapy/diagnosis or medical advice.
- Clear opt-in for any feedback; do **not** gather private journal content, exact behavioral logs, health conditions or identifying records by default.
- Explain that debug builds may be wiped, no data sync is configured, and uninstall may erase data. Do not invite personal writing until tested export/restore and migration controls exist.
- Participants may withdraw or delete local data without penalty. Offer a safe contact method controlled by the product owner; do not invent contact details.

## Prerequisites before a real pilot
- A verified installable beta built from reviewed source.
- Native visual and basic accessibility approval by the owner.
- Local export + restore + erase passed emulator and manual tests across reinstall/upgrade.
- Reminder opt-in, permission handling and no-notification paths verified, or explicitly removed from pilot MVP scope.
- Privacy disclosures and a secure, consent-based way to collect only aggregate usability feedback.
- User-defined recruitment method and public/beta invitation approval.

## Suggested usability sessions
1. Fresh start: understand product promise, select/create growth area, set one small activity challenge.
2. Log completed, partial, rested and explicit missed; explain unreported clearly.
3. Modify tomorrow's action and recurrence; verify historic records remain unchanged.
4. Pause a challenge, resume, and interpret calendar day versus session targets.
5. Record and edit a private reflection with optional mood and energy.
6. Export a file to chosen storage, restore it after reinstall or local reset, verify fidelity; explicitly erase if desired.
7. Choose reminders, decline notification permission, grant it later, and cancel a paused challenge alert.
8. Use TalkBack / large font and repeat navigation with accessibility enabled.

## What to record (minimal and anonymized)
- Completion of each scripted task without assistance; confusing steps and accessibility blockers.
- User-rated clarity and calmness; reported pressure/shame or privacy concerns.
- Objective technical defects: lost records, incorrect counts, stale reminders, crashes, Android version/device only.
- Note when a test is **not observed**, not as “passed.”
- Do not claim the pilot validates psychological efficacy or a fixed habit formation period.

## Pilot exit gate
- All high-severity privacy, data-loss, incorrect progression and accessibility defects resolved.
- Repeatable data migration and process-death tests green.
- Product owner reviews the anonymized pilot findings and explicitly approves a signed release candidate.
