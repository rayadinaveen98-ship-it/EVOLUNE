# EVOLUNE v1 — Locked MVP scope

**Decision:** 2026-11-11. The product owner approved the existing product and visual direction and authorized implementation of outstanding work, subject to verified engineering and release gates.

**North star:** A calmer way to become better. Progress without pressure, discipline without punishment, growth without comparison.

## Intended user
Adults exploring sustainable, self-directed personal growth in everyday domains. NOT a mental-health treatment, therapy, diagnosis, or clinical assessment product. This audience definition is a **product decision**, not evidence that target-user research has been completed.

## Version 1 core experience (in scope)
1. Gentle, optional first-run introduction and selection/customization of a meaningful growth domain.
2. Create, edit and archive independent challenges: calendar days or completed sessions; custom goals, frequencies, local cues and fallback actions.
3. Daily Today view, honest logging (completed, backup, partial, intentionally rested, explicitly missed, unreported, paused), timeline and milestone review.
4. Recovery/revision without changing or erasing past history; pause behavior, timezone-aware dates and scheduled opportunities.
5. Private journal with optional self-reported mood and energy; transparent factual Insights, no self-worth scoring.
6. User-controlled local export, clearly confirmed deletion and a tested restore or migration pathway before the app encourages reliance on irreplaceable personal writing.
7. User-controlled optional local reminder notifications with permissions, update/cancel/reboot/timezone handling.
8. Stillwater + Atelier original art, clear type, accessible responsive Compose layouts, restrained/reduced motion.
9. Production-grade local-first privacy, crash/error handling, accessibility, device QA, realistic user pilot and release review.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1
- Healthcare treatment claims, diagnosis, emergency intervention
- Public social feed, competitive leaderboards, punitive streaks
- Broad AI coach, autonomous life decisions, complex rewards economy
- Paid API/hosting dependency or mandatory online account
- A separate complete exercise training or reading product

## Exit criteria
- Every in-scope flow implemented and tested on real Android; no fake states.
- Validated export/restore after process death and reinstall; deletion is deliberate and complete.
- No automatic “unreported = missed” or “21 days = formed habit” logic.
- User visual approval based on native Android screenshots and interaction review.
- Limited consenting pilot completed; test observations and issues recorded with outcomes.
- Release checklist and privacy/security review completed; signed AAB/APK prepared and approved by product owner before publication.

## Approval limits
“Consider everything approved” authorizes working on agreed plans and visuals; it does **not** establish real-world usability evidence, authorize Play Store publication, spending, use of real people's personal data, or access to the owner’s signing keys. External release and pilot steps remain explicit human gates.

## Baseline
HTML interactive v0.3 is the approved visual/UX direction. Android native v0.5 is the current implementation baseline; PR #2 is under review.
