# Product brief — v0.1 (proposal)

**Project:** EVOLUNE (working title)  
**Status:** Discovery; not a locked feature specification.

## Why it exists
Help people build a personally meaningful, sustainable path to mental and physical self-development. They should be able to take challenges, understand patterns, reflect clearly, and continue despite imperfect days.

**North-star experience:** Open the app, know what matters today, act on a small meaningful step, and feel informed—not judged—when returning.

## Audience and user needs
- A beginner who wants to start without feeling overwhelmed.
- A returning user who struggles with consistency, interruptions, or excessive plans.
- A committed user who wants a transparent, customisable growth plan and history.
- A privacy-conscious user who wants control over personal reflections and records.

Do not assume every user is seeking clinical mental-health care or weight loss.

## Core behavior loop (proposed)
1. **Explore** — user chooses development areas and explains why they matter.
2. **Choose** — user selects or authors one meaningful, realistic challenge.
3. **Plan** — tiny action + frequency + when/where + a backup plan.
4. **Act** — focus on the actual behavior, not opening the app.
5. **Reflect** — optional short feedback on perceived difficulty, mood, energy, circumstances.
6. **Adapt** — review and adjust sustainably; pausing is a legitimate choice.
7. **Continue** — graduate from finite challenge to ongoing practice if wanted.

## Candidate pillars (not all MVP commitments)
- **Growth Map:** a personally selected set of domains, values, and intentions.
- **Challenge Studio:** customizable 7/21/30/60/90-day (or open-ended) challenges.
- **Daily Rhythm:** simple today view and user-owned reminders.
- **Reflection:** optional journaling, subjective mood/energy check-ins and insights.
- **Recovery Mode:** pause, resume, reduce scope, or reset a plan without shaming.
- **Living World:** quiet artistic representation of progress without extrinsic pressure.
- **Knowledge Space:** clearly labelled evidence-informed explanations and guided practices.

## Defining moment: missed days
A missed day is recorded honestly, never erased to claim perfect adherence. The user can choose a smaller next action, reschedule, pause, or resume. Avoid automatic punitive streak resets or manipulative retention tactics.

## Candidate example
Goal: 'Feel more active.'  
User-chosen challenge: 'Walk 15 minutes after breakfast on weekdays for 21 days.'  
If-then plan: 'If breakfast ends, put on shoes and walk. If it's raining, walk indoors for 5 minutes instead.'  
Check-in: completed / partially completed / missed / intentionally rested; optional notes.  
Weekly review: actual adherence, barriers, perceived effort, modifications.  
At day 21: review the experience and optionally continue. Do **not** label it a scientifically formed habit.

## Out-of-scope for initial MVP
- Diagnosis, clinical treatment, crisis services, medical recommendations.
- Automatic psychological diagnoses based on mood logs.
- Mandatory social competition, shame-based streak mechanics.
- Extensive AI coaching or a paid third-party dependency without evaluation.
- A complete workout-training system (a separate app concept exists).

## Candidate success measures
- Users can create a meaningful challenge in under ~3 minutes (design target, not tested).
- Users can understand 'what next?' without hunting across categories.
- Challenge setup completion; return-to-practice after interruption; user-reported helpfulness; week 4 retention.
- Avoid optimizing primarily for notifications, screen time, or streak length.

## Open decisions
Target users and market; first three development domains; exact scope of reflection and nutrition; local-first/sync strategy; monetization; brand and art direction; challenge complexity. These require design review and research before locking.
